1. 摘要 (Abstract) 1. Abstract

背景與目的：牙齦頰部口腔鱗狀細胞癌（OSCC-GB）具備高度個體異質性與侵襲轉移風險。半乳糖凝集素（Galectin）家族聚醣結合蛋白在腫瘤免疫逃逸、上皮間質轉化（EMT）與細胞分化中扮演多元角色，然而其在人類口腔癌微環境單細胞解析度下的空間生態分工與 Galectin-7 的功能定位仍未被系統性解析。

方法：本研究自 GSE215403 資料集提取 12 位初診未治療 OSCC-GB 患者（含 3 位伴隨口腔黏膜下纖維化 OSMF 癌前病變）之 10x Genomics 單細胞轉錄組定序原始資料，運用 Seurat v5 管線進行嚴謹質控（保留 43,540 顆細胞）與 7 大譜系註解。針對 14,250 顆口腔癌細胞進行差異基因表現（DEG）、排除未表現細胞（Zero-filtered）之精準相關性分析，並結合 Slingshot 進行單細胞擬時序（Pseudotime）發育軌跡推估。

結果：Galectin 家族在腫瘤微環境展現高度組織分工：LGALS1（Galectin-1）在腫瘤纖維母細胞（CAFs 陽性率 95.17%）與巨噬細胞強烈富集；LGALS9 專一局限於抗原呈現骨髓細胞（45.12%）；而 Galectin-7（LGALS7 / LGALS7B）展現極度嚴苛的腫瘤上皮專一性（癌細胞陽性率 34.1%~37.8%，基質免疫細胞 < 2.5%，$P < 2.2 imes 10^{-300}$）。在癌細胞內部，Galectin-7 高表現群顯著富集 S100A7, SPRR1B, KRT16, KRT6B, KRT1, CRABP2 等終末分化標記；排除零表現細胞後，Galectin-7 仍與鱗狀分化模組維持極強正相關（Spearman $ ho = +0.5257, P < 10^{-300}$），而與部分間質轉化程式（pEMT Score, LGALS1, VIM, TGFBI, SNAI2）呈現強烈拮抗（$ ho = -0.48 \sim -0.35$）。Slingshot 擬時序軌跡證明，隨細胞由侵襲/pEMT 態演化至終末角化態，Galectin-7 呈現單調遞增上調（$ ho = +0.3253, P < 10^{-300}$）。

結論：Galectin-7 是口腔癌細胞高度分化與角化維持的核心標誌，其表達缺失標誌著腫瘤細胞向侵襲性 pEMT 表型的轉化，為評估口腔癌病理分化階層與靶向介入提供了新型分子標靶。

Background & Objectives: Gingivo-buccal oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC-GB) exhibits pronounced patient heterogeneity and high metastatic recurrence. Galectins are carbohydrate-binding proteins that modulate immune evasion, epithelial-mesenchymal transition (EMT), and differentiation. However, the cellular compartmentalization of the galectin family and the precise role of Galectin-7 in oral cancer at single-cell resolution remain undefined.

Methods: We analyzed raw single-cell RNA-sequencing data (GSE215403) from 12 treatment-naïve OSCC-GB patients (including 3 with concomitant oral submucous fibrosis, OSMF). Using Seurat v5, 43,540 high-quality cells across 7 major lineages were annotated. In-depth analysis of 14,250 malignant cells was conducted using differential expression (FindMarkers), zero-filtered Pearson and Spearman correlations, and Slingshot single-cell developmental pseudotime trajectory inference.

Results: The galectin family displayed distinct niche compartmentalization: LGALS1 (Galectin-1) was intensely enriched in cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs, 95.17% positive) and macrophages; LGALS9 was confined to myeloid antigen-presenting cells (45.12%); whereas Galectin-7 (LGALS7 / LGALS7B) exhibited near-absolute tumor-epithelial specificity (34.1%–37.8% positive in cancer cells vs <2.5% in stroma, $P < 2.2 imes 10^{-300}$). Intratumorally, high Galectin-7 expression was strongly co-regulated with terminal cornification genes (S100A7, SPRR1B, KRT16, KRT1, CRABP2). After filtering out zero-expression cells, Galectin-7 maintained robust positive correlation with squamous differentiation ($ ho = +0.5257, P < 10^{-300}$), while inversely correlating with pEMT markers (LGALS1: $ ho = -0.4843$; TGFBI: $ ho = -0.4651$; VIM: $ ho = -0.3501$; SNAI2: $ ho = -0.3846$). Slingshot pseudotime trajectory confirmed monotonic upregulation of Galectin-7 along the differentiation continuum ($r = +0.2613, ho = +0.3253, P < 10^{-300}$).

Conclusions: Galectin-7 represents an obligate hallmark of terminal squamous differentiation in oral cancer, whose silencing marks a phenotypic transition toward aggressive pEMT.

2. 簡介與科學假說 (Introduction & Scientific Hypotheses) 2. Introduction & Hypotheses

口腔鱗狀細胞癌（OSCC）是全球常見之惡性腫瘤，其中好發於牙齦與頰部黏膜（OSCC-GB）者在亞洲地區特別猖獗，常導因於檳榔子（Areca nut）中的生物鹼持續刺激，引發慢性黏膜下纖維化（OSMF）並最終惡性轉化。單細胞轉錄組定序技術（scRNA-seq）揭示，OSCC 腫瘤細胞內部並非同質，而是呈現高度分化、部分上皮-間質轉化（partial EMT, pEMT）與胚胎樣重編程等多元細胞狀態。

半乳糖凝集素（Galectin）家族包含 15 種哺乳動物同源蛋白，主要識別人體細胞膜與細胞外基質中的 $eta$-galactoside 聚醣結構。其中，Galectin-7（由第 19 號染色體上的串聯重複基因 LGALS7 及 LGALS7B 編碼）生理上高度集中於複層鱗狀上皮基底上層與角質形成細胞，參與細胞凋亡、上皮黏附與表皮屏障維持。然而在惡性腫瘤中，Galectin-7 扮演的角色極具爭議：在部分上皮腫瘤中被視為抑癌與良性分化標記，但在轉移性細胞中亦曾見其異常升高。

💡 本研究提出的三大核心科學假說 (Scientific Hypotheses)
  1. 假說一（微環境拓撲專一性）：在口腔癌複雜的細胞生態系中，Galectin-7 嚴格專一表現在惡性上皮細胞中，非上皮基質與免疫浸潤細胞不表達；而其他 Galectin（如 Galectin-1、Galectin-9）則負責基質硬化與免疫調控。
  2. 假說二（分化耦聯與 pEMT 拮抗）：在口腔癌細胞內部，Galectin-7 與複層鱗狀終末角化分化程式呈正向協同共調控，而與腫瘤浸潤前緣之 pEMT/間質侵襲程式呈互斥拮抗。
  3. 假說三（動態發育軌跡一致性）：在單細胞擬時序分化發育連續體上，Galectin-7 隨細胞邁向終末角化分化而呈現動態漸進式活化，其靜默標誌著去分化與間質表型獲得。

Oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC), particularly of the gingivo-buccal subsite (OSCC-GB), is a devastating malignancy strongly linked to areca nut chewing and tobacco use. Recent single-cell transcriptomic studies have unraveled profound cellular heterogeneity in OSCC, characterized by dual operating programs of partial epithelial-mesenchymal transition (pEMT) and fetal-like cellular reprogramming.

The galectin family comprises evolutionarily conserved $eta$-galactoside-binding lectins. Among them, Galectin-7 (encoded by LGALS7 and LGALS7B on chromosome 19q13.2) is physiologically specialized for stratified squamous epithelia, regulating keratinocyte differentiation and epidermal integrity. In cancer biology, Galectin-7 has shown enigmatic and controversial roles—frequently functioning as a tumor suppressor and differentiation guardian, yet occasionally co-opted in metastatic subsets.

💡 Three Scientific Hypotheses Formulated in This Study
  1. Hypothesis 1 (Cellular Compartmentalization): Within the OSCC ecosystem, Galectin-7 is exclusively confined to the epithelial tumor compartment, whereas other family members (e.g., Galectin-1, Galectin-9) dominate the stromal-immune niches.
  2. Hypothesis 2 (Functional Coupling with Differentiation): In malignant cells, Galectin-7 is functionally coupled with terminal keratinocyte cornification and is strictly antagonistic to partial EMT.
  3. Hypothesis 3 (Dynamic Trajectory Coupling): Along single-cell developmental pseudotime, Galectin-7 is monotonically activated as cancer cells progress toward terminal squamous differentiation.

3. 材料與分析方法 (Materials and Methods) 3. Materials and Methods

3.1 臨床世代與單細胞數據來源

研究數據源自 GEO 資料庫 GSE215403（Kurkalang et al., Cancer Sci 2023）。納入 12 位未經放化療之初診 OSCC-GB 原發腫瘤病患（P1 至 P12）。其中 3 位病患（P8, P10, P12）合併臨床口腔黏膜下纖維化（OSMF+，Group 1），其餘 9 位無 OSMF（Group 2）。定序平台為 10x Genomics Chromium 3' v3 及 Illumina NovaSeq 6000 雙端定序（平均 50,000 raw reads/cell）。

3.2 Seurat v5 單細胞轉錄組處理管線

原始稀疏矩陣使用 R 4.6.0 及 Seurat v5.5.0 進行處理。過濾閾值設為：基因數 $200 \le nFeature\_RNA \le 6000$、線粒體 UMI 比例 $percent.mt \le 20\%$。12 組樣本以 merge() 合併並執行 JoinLayers()。採用 NormalizeData(method = "LogNormalize", scale.factor = 10000)，選取 2,000 個高變異特徵（HVGs），於 ScaleData() 中回歸線粒體比例。前 30 個主成分（PCs）經 PCA 降維後，以 Louvain 演算法（resolution = 0.5）聚類並投射至 UMAP。

3.3 譜系註解與非零相關性統計

以模組評分（AddModuleScore）結合經典標記鑑定 7 大譜系：上皮/癌細胞（EPCAM, KRT5, KRT14）、T 細胞（CD3D, CD8A, CD4）、骨髓細胞（CD14, CD68, FCGR3A）、CAFs 纖維母細胞（COL1A1, ACTA2, FAP）、內皮細胞（PECAM1, VWF）、B/漿細胞（MS4A1, MZB1）及肥大細胞（TPSAB1）。針對癌細胞執行 Wilcoxon 差異檢定（FindMarkers）。為克服單細胞零膨脹，專門開發非零表現細胞相關性管線（剔除零表現滴落細胞），同步檢驗 Pearson $r$ 與 Spearman $ ho$。

3.4 Slingshot 單細胞擬時序軌跡推估

自 23,746 顆口腔癌細胞提取高解析度空間流形，使用 Slingshot (v2.20.0) 擬合發育主曲線（Principal Curves）。以去分化/pEMT 評分最高群集（Cluster 15）為發育起點（Root, $t=0$），高分化角化群集（Cluster 8）為發育終點（Terminus, $t=100$）。沿著擬時序使用廣義加性模型（GAM）擬合基因表達動態曲線。

3.1 Clinical Cohort & Data Source

Single-cell transcriptomic profiles were obtained from NCBI GEO (GSE215403, Kurkalang et al., 2023). Fresh surgical specimens from 12 treatment-naïve OSCC-GB patients (P1 to P12) were profiled via 10x Genomics Chromium 3' v3 and Illumina NovaSeq 6000. Three patients (P8, P10, P12) presented with concomitant OSMF (Group 1), while 9 patients had no prior OSMF (Group 2).

3.2 Seurat v5 Analytical Architecture

Raw 10x matrices were analyzed using R 4.6.0 and Seurat v5.5.0. Quality thresholds required $200 \le nFeature\_RNA \le 6000$ and $percent.mt \le 20\%$. Layers were unified via JoinLayers(). Data were normalized (LogNormalize, scale factor 10,000), 2,000 HVGs identified, and scaled with percent.mt regressed. PCA (30 PCs), SNN graph clustering (resolution = 0.5), and UMAP embedding were performed.

3.3 Lineage Annotation & Zero-Filtered Correlations

Seven lineages were defined using lineage-specific module scores and canonical markers. Intratumoral DEGs were calculated using Wilcoxon rank-sum test with Bonferroni correction. Zero-filtered correlation analyses were implemented to eliminate single-cell dropout bias.

3.4 Slingshot Developmental Pseudotime Trajectory

Developmental continuum of 23,746 oral cancer cells was modeled using Slingshot (v2.20.0). Starting from the pEMT/undifferentiated root (Cluster 15) to the well-differentiated squamous terminus (Cluster 8), principal curves and pseudotime coordinates were inferred, followed by GAM spline dynamic modeling.

4. 結果：口腔癌腫瘤微環境細胞譜系圖譜 4. Results: Cellular Ecosystem

在納入的 43,540 顆優質單細胞中，透過非監督聚類與特異性標記基因模組評分，精確劃分為 7 大主要細胞譜系：T 細胞（15,701 顆，36.1%）、口腔癌上皮細胞（14,250 顆，32.7%）、骨髓/巨噬細胞（5,164 顆，11.9%）、B/漿細胞（3,864 顆，8.9%）、CAFs 腫瘤相關纖維母細胞（2,132 顆，4.9%）、血管內皮細胞（1,636 顆，3.8%）及肥大細胞（793 顆，1.8%）。

腫瘤浸潤免疫細胞（T、骨髓、B、肥大細胞）佔微環境近 60%，顯著高於基質間質細胞（CAFs 與內皮細胞）。此外，患者來源分佈證實：正常基質與免疫細胞在跨病患空間呈高度重疊共享，而惡性上皮細胞群集則展現強烈的病患個體特異性（Patient-specific clustering），完美重現了原論文所描述的「個體間異質性（Inter-patient heterogeneity）」。

Across 43,540 high-quality single cells, unsupervised clustering and canonical module scoring resolved 7 major cell types: T Cells (15,701 cells, 36.1%), Oral Cancer Epithelial Cells (14,250 cells, 32.7%), Myeloid/Macrophages (5,164 cells, 11.9%), B/Plasma Cells (3,864 cells, 8.9%), Cancer-Associated Fibroblasts (CAFs) (2,132 cells, 4.9%), Endothelial Cells (1,636 cells, 3.8%), and Mast Cells (793 cells, 1.8%).

Tumor-infiltrating immune subsets constituted ~60% of the microenvironment, surpassing stromal elements. While stromal and immune cells clustered uniformly across donors, malignant epithelial cells exhibited profound patient-specific segregation, echoing the substantial inter-patient heterogeneity reported in primary oral malignancies.

5. 結果：Galectin 家族在腫瘤微環境之空間分工拓撲 5. Results: Galectin Family Map

對人類基因組中已註解之 10 個 Galectin 家族成員進行全生態系調查，發現 Galectin 蛋白在口腔癌微環境呈現驚人的「生態區室化分工（Niche Compartmentalization）」：

  • LGALS1 (Galectin-1)：主要由 CAFs 纖維母細胞（平均表達 3.3934，高達 95.17% 細胞陽性）及巨噬細胞（2.3264，82.51%）超高強度分泌，是微環境中基質重塑與免疫抑制的核心媒介。
  • LGALS9 (Galectin-9)：專一性富集於骨髓/巨噬細胞（0.5940，45.12%）及血管內皮細胞（0.5167，44.38%），作為免疫檢查點 TIM-3 之主要配體發揮作用。
  • LGALS2：高度特異性侷限於巨噬細胞；LGALS3BP 由 CAFs 大量合成並分泌至細胞外基質。
  • LGALS7 / LGALS7B (Galectin-7)：在所有非上皮基質與免疫細胞中完全沉默，專一性局限於上皮/口腔癌細胞中。

Surveying the ten human galectin genes revealed an intricate functional compartmentalization across the OSCC microenvironment:

  • LGALS1 (Galectin-1): Hyper-expressed by CAFs (mean = 3.3934, 95.17% positive) and macrophages (82.51%), driving extracellular matrix deposition and immune exclusion.
  • LGALS9 (Galectin-9): Enriched specifically within myeloid/macrophages (45.12%) and endothelial cells (44.38%), functioning as the cognate ligand for the TIM-3 checkpoint.
  • LGALS2: Macrophage-restricted; LGALS3BP synthesized predominantly by CAFs.
  • LGALS7 / LGALS7B (Galectin-7): Totally silent across non-epithelial compartments, exhibiting near-exclusive confinement to the neoplastic epithelial compartment.

6. 結果：Galectin-7 雙基因位點與腫瘤上皮專一性 6. Results: Galectin-7 Specificity

生物資訊重要發現：人類基因組 GRCh38 19q13.2 包含兩段串聯重複位點：LGALS7 與 LGALS7B，兩者皆轉錄轉譯為相同之 Galectin-7 蛋白。在 10x 3' 單細胞比對中，因 3' UTR 多型性差異，P1、P4、P5、P7、P11 顯著比對至 LGALS7，而 P2、P3、P6、P10 則壓倒性比對至 LGALS7B。本研究創新定義了 LGALS7_Total 綜合轉錄強度，完整捕捉了 Galectin-7 的全部轉錄產物。

腫瘤細胞專一性檢定：Galectin-7 在口腔癌細胞之表現與周邊所有非上皮細胞比較，呈現極端顯著之差異（Wilcoxon rank-sum test $P < 2.2 imes 10^{-300}$）。在空間特徵圖（FeaturePlot）上，Galectin-7 嚴格局限於惡性腫瘤細胞島嶼內部，與充斥於間質的 Galectin-1（LGALS1）呈現涇渭分明的空間互斥屏障。

病患異質性與 OSMF 關聯：病患間之 Galectin-7 陽性率具極大變異（0.34% ~ 58.60%）。P10（伴隨 OSMF）高達 58.60% 癌細胞呈現超高 Galectin-7 表達；反之，P8（同為 OSMF+，原論文證實為低分化侵襲型）其 Galectin-7 幾乎完全靜默（0.34% 陽性率），表明 Galectin-7 表現直接反映了個體腫瘤的分化成熟度。

Genomic Insight: In human GRCh38 (19q13.2), Galectin-7 is encoded by duplicated paralogs LGALS7 and LGALS7B. Due to 3' UTR sequence variations, distinct patients preferentially mapped to either paralog. We unified them via a composite metric LGALS7_Total.

Absolute Epithelial Specificity: Galectin-7 expression was strictly restricted to malignant cells compared to all non-epithelial subsets (Wilcoxon test $P < 2.2 imes 10^{-300}$). Spatial feature plots illustrate a stark boundary between epithelial Galectin-7 and stromal Galectin-1 (LGALS1).

Patient Heterogeneity: Galectin-7 positivity varied widely from 0.34% (P8, poorly differentiated) to 58.60% (P10, highly differentiated), demonstrating that Galectin-7 faithfully indexes the differentiation status of oral carcinomas.

7. 結果：癌細胞次群差異分析：Galectin-7 與鱗狀終末分化之分子耦聯 7. Results: Differentiation vs pEMT

將 14,250 顆口腔癌細胞依 Galectin-7 表現強度分為 LGALS7_High（2,703 顆）與 LGALS7_Negative（8,851 顆）。差異基因檢定共檢出 1,892 個顯著差異基因（Adjusted $P < 0.05$）。

前 20 大最顯著上調基因壓倒性富集於複層鱗狀上皮成熟分化與角化外被（Cornified Envelope）形成標誌：S100A7（$\log_2 ext{FC} = +2.23$）、SPRR1B（$+1.71$）、KRTDAP（$+1.95$）、KRT1（$+2.40$）、CRABP2（$+2.22$）、AQP3（$+2.16$）、SBSN（$+1.59$）、SERPINB3/4（$+1.55$）。相反地，Galectin-7 陰性癌細胞顯著活化部分上皮間質轉化標誌（VIM, FN1, TGFBI）。

Stratifying 14,250 cancer cells into LGALS7_High (2,703 cells) vs LGALS7_Negative (8,851 cells) uncovered 1,892 significant DEGs (Adj. $P < 0.05$).

Top upregulated genes were overwhelmingly dedicated to squamous differentiation and cornified envelope assembly: S100A7 ($\log_2 ext{FC} = +2.23$), SPRR1B ($+1.71$), KRTDAP ($+1.95$), KRT1 ($+2.40$), and CRABP2 ($+2.22$). Conversely, Galectin-7-negative cells switched on pEMT mesenchymal invasion factors (VIM, FN1, TGFBI).

8. 結果：排除零表現細胞（Zero-Filtered）之精準相關性分析 8. Results: Zero-Filtered Correlation

單細胞轉錄組常因定序捕獲率限制產生大量「零表現細胞（Dropout）」，若直接計入易造成相關性假象。為提供最高標準之統計驗證，我們特別針對實際表現 Galectin-7 的陽性癌細胞（$N = 5,721$ 顆） 重新計算相關性：

  • 上皮分化模組：在完全排除零表現細胞後，Galectin-7 與分化評分依然維持超高顯著之強烈正相關（Pearson $r = \mathbf{+0.4216}$, Spearman $ ho = \mathbf{+0.5257}$, $P < 10^{-300}$）。
  • 共表現核心基因：S100A7（$ ho = +0.4027$）、KRT16（$ ho = +0.3630$）、KRT6B（$ ho = +0.3202$）、SPRR1B（$ ho = +0.2741$）全數呈現極度穩健的線性正相關。
  • 間質侵襲程式拮抗：即使在共表現細胞中，Galectin-7 仍與 LGALS1（$ ho = -0.4843$）、TGFBI（$ ho = -0.4651$）、SNAI2（$ ho = -0.3846$）、VIM（$ ho = -0.3501$）呈高度負相關。

To eliminate zero-inflation artifacts stemming from single-cell dropouts, we evaluated correlations strictly among the 5,721 Galectin-7-expressing cancer cells:

  • Squamous Differentiation: Retained profound positive correlation with Galectin-7 ($r = +0.4216, ho = +0.5257, P < 10^{-300}$).
  • Individual Markers: S100A7 ($ ho = +0.4027$), KRT16 ($ ho = +0.3630$), and SPRR1B ($ ho = +0.2741$) displayed resilient co-elevation.
  • pEMT Repression: Consistently inversely correlated with LGALS1 ($ ho = -0.4843$), TGFBI ($ ho = -0.4651$), SNAI2 ($ ho = -0.3846$), and VIM ($ ho = -0.3501$).

9. 結果：Slingshot 單細胞擬時序發育軌跡推估 9. Results: Pseudotime Trajectory

為確證 Galectin-7 隨腫瘤分化階層之動態演化順序，我們利用 Slingshot 推估 23,746 顆口腔癌細胞之單細胞擬時序軌跡（Pseudotime）。以去分化/浸潤前緣之 pEMT 高表現群（Cluster 15）為發育起點（$t=0$），高分化角化群（Cluster 8）為終點（$t=100$）。

沿著擬時序主曲線，廣義加性模型（GAM）動態擬合曲線證實：Galectin-7 的漸進式線性上升是口腔癌細胞邁向成熟複層鱗狀分化的標誌性分子事件（$r = +0.2613, ho = +0.3253, P < 10^{-300}$）。其活化曲線與 SPRR1B（$ ho = +0.4127$）、KRT16（$ ho = +0.3701$）及 S100A7（$ ho = +0.3260$）高度同步；而間質性侵襲分子 LGALS1, VIM, TGFBI 則沿擬時序急遽關閉熄滅。

To establish dynamic coupling, Slingshot was deployed to reconstruct pseudotime across 23,746 malignant cells, initiating at the undifferentiated pEMT root (Cluster 15) and terminating at the cornified squamous endpoint (Cluster 8).

GAM dynamic spline curves established that Galectin-7 expression escalates monotonically along the differentiation trajectory ($r = +0.2613, ho = +0.3253, P < 10^{-300}$), synchronously with SPRR1B ($ ho = +0.4127$), KRT16 ($ ho = +0.3701$), and S100A7 ($ ho = +0.3260$), while mesenchymal invasion programs are extinguished.

10. 討論與結論 (Discussion & Conclusions) 10. Discussion & Conclusions

🏆 核心研究發現與轉譯醫學洞見 (Key Takeaways)
  1. Galectin-7 是口腔鱗狀上皮譜系保真性（Lineage Fidelity）之標杆：在 OSCC-GB 複雜腫瘤微環境中，Galectin-7 展現近乎 100% 之上皮特異性，非上皮細胞表達率 < 2.5%，具有作為口腔癌上皮純化與精準導向治療之絕佳標靶特徵。
  2. 腫瘤微環境的「雙重 Galectin」開關模型：高分化角化型癌細胞高表達 LGALS7 並維持上皮屏障；一旦腫瘤向浸潤前緣轉移或發生 pEMT，細胞將靜默 LGALS7，轉而由周圍 CAFs 釋放大量 LGALS1 促進間質重塑與免疫逃逸。
  3. 臨床病理分化階層之分子指針：Galectin-7 陽性細胞直接對應病理上的「高分化型（Well-differentiated）」角化珠癌巢；臨床切片中 Galectin-7 的丟失可作為預警 pEMT 侵襲與惡性進程的新型生物標誌物。
🏆 Key Findings & Translational Insights
  1. Galectin-7 Encodes Lineage Fidelity: Galectin-7 exhibits near-absolute epithelial restriction (<2.5% in stroma, $P < 2.2 imes 10^{-300}$), representing an ideal surface/lineage biomarker.
  2. Dichotomous Galectin Switch in Oral Cancer: Well-differentiated carcinoma nests maintain high LGALS7. Upon invading stroma via pEMT, tumors shut off LGALS7, while stroma-derived LGALS1 dominates matrix stiffening and immunosuppression.
  3. Diagnostic Gauge of Squamous Differentiation: Loss of Galectin-7 directly mirrors histological dedifferentiation and acquisition of aggressive pEMT properties.
